What’s new in 1.2.1

  • Pinch-to-zoom now scales the page or photo around your fingers, instead of only panning.
  • Double-tap zoom is on in Read mode: first tap zooms in around the tap, second tap returns to normal size.
  • After you zoom, one finger pans in both directions. In Pen or Fill, one finger still draws or places a field, and two fingers pinch-zoom.

Also included

  • Undo works right after you edit. PDFs have Fill for signature, initials, date, and auto-fill from a profile saved on the phone.
  • Photos, audio playback, a light Excel sheet, and visible edit controls.

Fill stays in the app and is not a certified e-signature. Built for phones like the Galaxy S24 Ultra (Android 12 and newer, 64-bit).
