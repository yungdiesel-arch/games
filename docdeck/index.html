What's new in 1.11.0

  • Scan to PDF: take a photo or pick one, drag the corners, straighten, choose Color, Grayscale or B&W, rotate, reorder pages, and save a PDF.
  • Recognize text (English, works offline): copy it, read it aloud, save it as a note, or make a scanned PDF searchable.
  • About 30 more sheet formulas, like MEDIAN, AVERAGEIFS, TEXTJOIN, SUBSTITUTE, EOMONTH and PMT, with name suggestions and argument hints as you type.
  • PDF tools: extract pages, compress images, and add or remove a password.

Also in 1.10.1

  • Holding Backspace now deletes smoothly through paragraphs all the way to the top; the keyboard no longer stutters or stops at each line.
  • Enter splits paragraphs without the keyboard hiccuping.

Also in 1.10.0

  • Tap anywhere on a Word page in edit mode to put the cursor at the nearest text and open the keyboard. This works in the margins, between paragraphs, below the last line, in table cells, and at any zoom. The Edit button does it instantly.
  • Enter splits a paragraph, and Backspace joins paragraphs.
  • Type in Word table cells, and use Insert table to add a table.
  • Find and replace can match case, match whole words, and Replace all.
  • Word count shows words, characters, paragraphs, pages, and reading time.
  • Read aloud works for Word, PDF, and notes, with pause, skip, and speed.

Also in 1.8.1

  • Fit shows the real page with margins trimmed to fill the screen width, and zoom stays sharp.

Also in 1.8.0

  • PDF pages are drawn with the same PDF engine Chrome uses, so letters stay spaced the way they look in Adobe. Settings shows which PDF engine is active.

Also included

  • The Fit, Scroll, and exit buttons sit above the page.
  • In Fit mode, turn pages by scrolling up or down. The page number stays at the bottom, like 1/11, and the page fills more of the screen.
  • Documents open in Read. The top bar is icons only, and the top and bottom bars are solid and easier to see.
  • Full screen with Fit or Scroll (the continuous strip with page gutters). The last choice is saved.
  • A Scroll / Page toggle outside full screen, pinch-zoom, and immediate undo.
  • PDFs have Fill for signature, initials, date, and auto-fill from a profile saved on the phone.
  • Photos, audio playback, a light Excel sheet, and visible edit controls.

Fill stays in the app and is not a certified e-signature. Built for phones like the Galaxy S24 Ultra (Android 12 and newer, 64-bit).
