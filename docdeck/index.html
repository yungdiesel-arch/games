Download DocDeck 1.9.1
  1. Open the downloaded file on your phone.
  2. Allow “Install unknown apps” if asked.
  3. Tap Install.
  4. If Play Protect warns, tap “Install anyway.”
  5. This update installs over 1.9.0 or earlier. You don’t need to uninstall; your files and settings stay.

What's new in 1.9.1

  • Spreadsheets zoom from 25% to 400% with two fingers, in view and edit mode. Row numbers, column letters, and the selected cell stay lined up, and text stays sharp.
  • A zoom button shows the percentage. Tap it for Fit width, 50%, 75%, 100%, 150%, or 200%.
  • Word, slides, and photos pinch-zoom too, and double tap switches between fit and 200%.
  • Tap still selects a cell, and double tap edits it and now opens the keyboard. Each file remembers its zoom. PDF zoom is unchanged.

Also in 1.9.0

  • A fresh, modern look: clean Roboto text, white or dark backgrounds, and dark mode that follows your phone (or set it in Settings, Appearance).
  • Each file type has its own color: blue for Word, green for Excel, orange-red for slides, red for PDF. Long file names stay readable, with the type underneath.
  • Spreadsheets work like Google Sheets: a real grid with gridlines, a formula bar, number formats, a Format panel, insert and delete rows and columns, sort and filter, and sheet tabs you can add, rename, and delete.
  • Word documents show pages on a gray desk, with a formatting bar that stays above the keyboard when you edit. Slides show a thumbnail strip.
  • PDFs have separate View, Annotate, and Fill & Sign modes, so reading stays clean. Page display is unchanged from 1.8.1.

Also in 1.8.1

  • Fit shows the real page with margins trimmed to fill the screen width, and zoom stays sharp.

Also in 1.8.0

  • PDF pages are drawn with the same PDF engine Chrome uses, so letters stay spaced the way they look in Adobe. Settings shows which PDF engine is active.

Also included

  • The Fit, Scroll, and exit buttons sit above the page.
  • In Fit mode, turn pages by scrolling up or down. The page number stays at the bottom, like 1/11, and the page fills more of the screen.
  • Documents open in Read. The top bar is icons only, and the top and bottom bars are solid and easier to see.
  • Full screen with Fit or Scroll (the continuous strip with page gutters). The last choice is saved.
  • A Scroll / Page toggle outside full screen, pinch-zoom, and immediate undo.
  • PDFs have Fill for signature, initials, date, and auto-fill from a profile saved on the phone.
  • Photos, audio playback, a light Excel sheet, and visible edit controls.

Fill stays in the app and is not a certified e-signature. Built for phones like the Galaxy S24 Ultra (Android 12 and newer, 64-bit).
