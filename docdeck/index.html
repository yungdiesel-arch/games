DocDeck

Version 1.2.0 · 23 MB · arm64 only

Download DocDeck 1.2.0
  1. Open the downloaded file on your phone.
  2. Allow “Install unknown apps” if asked.
  3. Tap Install.
  4. If Play Protect warns, tap “Install anyway.”
  5. This update installs over 1.1.0. You don’t need to uninstall; your files and settings stay.

What’s new in 1.2.0

  • Undo works right after you edit, including while you are still using the pen. You don’t have to switch to Read first.
  • Pinch and double-tap zoom now scale the page or photo around your fingers. After you zoom, one finger pans in both directions.
  • Fill on PDFs: place a signature, initials, date, printed name, email, or text, then auto-fill from a profile saved on the phone. This stays in the app and is not a certified e-signature.

Also included

  • Open photos (JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, BMP, HEIC/HEIF) and play audio (MP3, M4A/AAC, WAV, OGG, FLAC).
  • Light Excel sheet with edit controls on screen. PDFs open on Pen. Word opens in the editor.
  • Pen and highlighter marks stay until you tap Undo or Eraser.
  • Offline library, PDF markup and forms, Word and Excel editing, slide view, and Save As.

Word editing is still basic text and formatting. Fill on Word and spreadsheets adds a signature line, not movable fields. Built for phones like the Galaxy S24 Ultra (Android 12 and newer, 64-bit).
