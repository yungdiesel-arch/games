  1. Open the downloaded file on your phone.

  5. This update installs over 1.0.0. You don’t need to uninstall; your files and settings stay.

What’s new in 1.1.0

  • Open photos: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, BMP, and HEIC/HEIF, with pinch and double-tap zoom.
  • Play audio: MP3, M4A/AAC, WAV, OGG, and FLAC, including from a notification after you leave the screen.
  • Scroll and pinch-zoom on documents and images.
  • Excel uses a light sheet (white cells, dark text). Edit cell, formats, and sheet controls are on screen.
  • Edit controls are visible when a document is opened. PDFs open on Pen. Word opens in the editor. Text files are labeled Edit text.
  • Pen and highlighter marks stay on the page until you tap Undo or Eraser.

Also included

  • Offline library: recents, favorites, search, open from storage, share-in from other apps, and download from a link.
  • PDF: view, search, copy text, pen, highlighter, eraser, undo/redo, fill forms, rotate, delete, reorder, and merge pages. Save and share.
  • Word (DOCX/ODT): accurate read view, plus text and basic formatting edits. Images and tables stay in the file.
  • Excel (XLSX/ODS/CSV): spreadsheet grid, common formulas including SUM, IF, VLOOKUP and XLOOKUP, sheets, frozen header, and number formats.
  • PowerPoint (PPTX/ODP): view and present, export to PDF. Slide editing comes later.
  • Text, Markdown, and JSON editor. Autosave, backups, and Save As (including into Drive or OneDrive through the system picker).

Word editing in this version is basic text and formatting. Complex layouts, live co-editing, and AI tools are not included. Built for phones like the Galaxy S24 Ultra (Android 12 and newer, 64-bit).
