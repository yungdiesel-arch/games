What's new in 1.3.0

  • Movie editor: up to 10 tracks — video, photo, GIF, music, voiceover, text and sticker.
  • Drag clips left/right (snaps to edges and playhead); long-press to move them to another track; drag ≡ to reorder tracks.
  • Colour-coded tracks with a legend; mute/hide, lock, add and delete per track.
  • Picture-in-picture overlays (upper tracks on top) with size, position and opacity; all audio tracks mixed in the export.
  • Old drafts upgrade automatically.

1.2.1

  • Movie maker preview fixed: it no longer stays black. The first frame shows right away, Play/Pause works, and the time and timeline move with playback. Dragging the timeline jumps to that spot.
  • The preview now plays your music and voiceover, follows each clip's speed and volume, and shows text and stickers.
  • If a clip can't be previewed, a red message explains why (tap it to retry) instead of a black screen.

1.2.0

  • New 🎬 Movie maker (in the Recordings tab): build a movie from videos and photos, with drafts that save automatically.
  • Timeline with tracks for clips, text, music and voiceover. Pinch to zoom, drag the trim handles, and use undo/redo.
  • Split, duplicate, move, delete and freeze-frame clips. Set speed from 0.25x to 4x, or a speed ramp, and adjust each clip's volume.
  • Photos: choose how long each one shows, with an optional Ken Burns pan and zoom.
  • Transitions: Fade, Flash, Slide, Wipe and Zoom.
  • Filters plus brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth and vignette.
  • Text with 5 fonts, colours, an optional background box and animations (fade, pop, slide up, typewriter), plus emoji stickers.
  • Opening title and ending card.
  • Background music with trim, volume, fade in/out and auto-ducking, plus voiceover recording.
  • Canvas sizes 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 and 4:5, with a blurred or black background fill.
  • Export in 720p, 1080p or 4K (on phones that support it) at 30 or 60 fps, with progress, then Open or Share.

1.1.0

  • Merging clips works: export now joins every clip into one video, even when the clips have different sizes, orientations, frame rates or no sound. Each clip is fitted to one frame with black bars where needed, and keeps its own trim, speed, crop and mute.
  • New ▶ Play all previews all your clips back-to-back.
  • New clip filmstrip with thumbnails, a clip count and the total length. Long-press and drag a clip to reorder it, or tap ✕ to remove it (with a confirmation, and Undo brings it back).
  • The preview no longer starts as a black screen.
  • The settings line has its own row and only shows what you changed, so nothing gets cut off. The toolbar fades at the edges so you can tell it scrolls, and buttons give a small vibration when tapped.
  • After exporting you can Open or Share the video right away.
  • Recordings list now shows the date, and the ⋯ button lets you rename or delete a recording.

1.0.9

  • Fixed a crash when tapping Split vocals (seen on Android 17 / Galaxy S24 Ultra). If Android won't allow background processing, the split now runs inside the app with on-screen progress.

1.0.8

  • Manual crop fixed: the crop box now maps exactly onto the video (letterbox bars and rotation accounted for), so what you frame is what you get.
  • Cropped preview keeps its true shape (no stretching or squashing) and can no longer go blank.
  • Export uses the same crop as the preview.
  • Vocal splitter no longer crashes making an instrumental: the AI model now downloads from a working host. If the download, AI engine, or memory fails, it falls back to Basic quality with a message. Mono audio gets a clear message, and preview and save errors show a friendly note.
